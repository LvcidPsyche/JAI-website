Privacy

Pre-launch summary · the full policy publishes at general availability.

JAI-Assay is in private beta. This page describes how we handle data today and the commitments we are building the product around. The binding, full-length privacy policy will replace this summary at public launch.

This website

The marketing site is a static page. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts. If you email us, we receive only what you choose to send.

Data you send to the API

Transient by default. Rows you submit for scoring are processed in memory. Only the aggregate quality report and per-row scores persist — your raw content is not retained unless you explicitly opt in with the X-JAI-Data-Handling: store header.
  • We never train on your data. Customer content is never used to train, fine-tune, or evaluate our own models — not even in aggregate. Our internal learning records operator metadata only, never your content.
  • Opt-in “store” mode is bounded. If you opt in, the raw payload is retained for 30 days (for large-job resume convenience), then pruned automatically. Scores and aggregate reports are kept.
  • Source documents are used only within the lifetime of the request that references them and are not persisted in transient mode.
  • Reproducibility without retention. The replication package lets you (or anyone) re-verify a result without us holding your data.
  • Enterprise customers can request a signed Data Processing Agreement.

What we will detail at launch

The full policy will cover the categories of data we process, legal bases, sub-processors, and how to exercise data-subject rights.
