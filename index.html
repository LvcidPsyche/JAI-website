The assay

Three judges. One published rubric. A result you can re-run.

Single-judge scores are a coin flip you can’t audit. JAI-Assay runs a panel and reports the agreement — the one number a competitor can’t fake.

Send your rows

Upload JAI-native, OpenAI chat, or HuggingFace formats. Add source documents to score faithfulness, or skip them — we’ll tell you what we couldn’t measure.

The panel scores

Three independent frontier models, each from a different lineage, score every row 1–5 on faithfulness, specificity, and utility against the published rubric — blind to one another, at temperature zero.

You get a verdict

Per-row scores, inter-judge agreement, a quality report, a certification mark, and a replication package so anyone can reproduce the result.

Certification marks

A mark that means something — because it’s reproducible.

What gets measured

Three dimensions, on a scale you can read.

01 · faithfulness

Does it stick to the evidence?

Every claim is checked against the source documents you provide. No sources, no faithfulness score — and we say so.

02 · specificity

Is it concrete and non-obvious?

Generic, hedged, or boilerplate answers score low. Sharp, particular, defensible answers score high.

03 · utility

Would training on it teach something?

Rewards rows that carry a transferable pattern worth learning, not filler that pads a row count.

∑ · the report

Bands, means, and disagreement.

Per-row bands, mean scores per dimension, and the rows your judges argued over most — surfaced, not hidden.

Why it holds up

The eval rig is the moat.

agreement

Statistics you can’t fake

Multi-judge agreement is reported on every run. Anyone can recompute it from the per-row scores — a single judge can’t produce it.

open rubric

Read exactly what we measure

The full rubric — dimensions, the 1–5 scale, calibration discipline — is published. No black-box scoring. Read the rubric →

replicate

Verify it yourself

Every audit ships a replication package. Re-run it through /v1/assay/replicate and compare, row by row.

tenant isolation

Your data stays yours

Transient by default — raw rows aren’t retained. We never train our models on customer content. Full stop.
